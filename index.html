Why sensitive data?

- Research uses data - Research can produce results that benefit society - However, a lot of data is unavailable for research because of (absolutely justified) privacy concerns - This means we miss out on benefit

Why TREs?

- A solution to that conflict - Better (more secure) than sharing data with individuals/teams - Can't leave a TRE on the train - Potential for standards and accreditation - Trust is more distributed (5 safes model?)

Security maximalism

- We need to handle sensitive data with care and respect - It is tempting to do everything you can do increase security - However - these improvements may hamper work - not all improvements are significant/worthwhile (security theatre) - _e.g._ periodic password reset

The reality

- **You cannot eliminate risk** - Every piece of work is a negotiation about what _acceptable_ risk is - Remember, there is value to the work otherwise you wouldn't do it at all - You need to balance the value of the outputs with the risk (severity likelihood) of disclosure
